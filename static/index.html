Model Architecture:

  • Primary: XGBoost (falls back to RandomForest if XGBoost unavailable)
  • Explainability: SHAP TreeExplainer (falls back to feature importance × delta)
  • Classes: low risk / mid risk / high risk
  • Features: Age, BP, Blood Sugar, Temp, Heart Rate, Pulse Pressure, MAP, BP Velocity, Weight Velocity, Family History, Proteinuria

Chunking is dosage-aware: a split is never allowed to land inside a dose expression, so a retrieved passage can never surface half a dose.
